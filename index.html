FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What kind of companies does NexVeins work with?
We work with growth-stage startups, mid-market companies and enterprises across SaaS, finance, logistics and healthcare. If you have repetitive processes or data trapped in silos, we can help.
How long does a typical project take?
Most automation and integration projects ship in 2–6 weeks. Custom AI copilots and larger platform builds typically run 2–4 months, with working software delivered incrementally.
Do you work with our existing tools?
Yes. We integrate with your current stack — CRMs, ERPs, Slack, Notion, data warehouses and more — rather than forcing you onto new platforms.
How do you handle data security?
Security is built in from day one: encrypted data handling, role-based access, private model deployments where required, and full NDAs on every engagement.
What does a free call include?
A 30-minute session where we map your biggest bottleneck, outline a technical approach, and give you a rough timeline and budget — yours to keep, whether you hire us or not.
